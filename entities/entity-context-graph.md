The queryable knowledge layer: typed nodes (Assertion/Entity/Artifact subclasses) connected by typed, tiered edges (provenance / structural / associative). Distinguished from a plain knowledge graph by attaching context directly to edges (confidence, proposer, status) rather than treating all links as equally authoritative facts.
